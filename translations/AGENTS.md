# Translation instructions

Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md). These rules apply to translated prose anywhere in the repository, including book/ and root introductions.

- Chinese is the initial manuscript source. Keep stable chapter and case identifiers across languages. Do not treat the repository name as an approved English book title.
- For a translated chapter, record the original file, source commit, and whether human review is pending in the translation's status note. Recheck affected translations when that source changes; do not call a stale pair synchronized.
- Apply the [book-writing skill](../.agents/skills/book-writing/SKILL.md) in the target language. Use the [terminology table](../docs/terminology.md) for concept alignment. Preserve conditions, actor distinctions, and checks; do not copy Chinese sentence structure or claim full STE compliance.
- Preserve qualifications, measurements, code literals, commands, and the distinction between a teaching scenario and observed results. Natural local phrasing is preferable to literal translation.
- Explain regional tools, formats, and work practices. Do not silently replace an untested local workflow with a claimed working alternative.
- Root introductions may be adapted summaries rather than line-by-line pairs. Agent instructions and tooling do not require bilingual duplicates. Add full translations only as real reviewed content becomes available.
- [Translation status](README.md) records available editions. Source review and reader comprehension remain manual checks; repository checks do not certify translation fidelity.
